Standalone Lit demo — npl-note

One self-contained file: the Lit 3 web component is compiled into this page (no CDN, no server, no build at view time). Open via file:// — double-click — and the custom element upgrades and behaves.

This note is upgraded by NplNote (Lit 3): check its data-npl-upgraded attribute and role="note".
Lit renders the collapsed-state summary and expands on click — the component owns the behavior, the light DOM keeps the text searchable and copyable.
